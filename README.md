"This three-day festive moleben is dedicated 
to the fifth Buddha of the good kalpa Maitreya (
Maidari), his early descent from the monastery of 
Tushita to the human continent of Jambudwipa. 
Maitreya (Maidari) is the Buddha of the coming good eon.

Lama Sopa Rinpoche notes: 
"The reading of Maitreya's mantras does 
not meet the instructions of Pabonka Rinpoche, 
but my desire that the Maitreya Project will 
succeed and bring the greatest benefit to 
living beings. 
If a person does not achieve 
enlightenment in the time of Buddha, 
then thanks to the practice of 
Maitreya, he will certainly achieve 
enlightenment at the time of the 
teachings of the Maitreya Buddha."
